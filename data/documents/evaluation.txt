Retrieval quality can be measured using Recall@K, Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR), and normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (nDCG). Recall@K checks whether relevant evidence appears in the first K results. MRR rewards a relevant result appearing at a high rank. nDCG accounts for the position of relevant results. Generation can additionally be evaluated for correctness, relevance, faithfulness, and groundedness.
